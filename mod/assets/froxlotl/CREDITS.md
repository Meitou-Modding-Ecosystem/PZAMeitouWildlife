# The froxlotl

- **Model, rig and colour textures: [TheIvoryDisaster](https://github.com/TheIvoryDisaster)**, made for PZA's mods (Meitou-Modding-Ecosystem/Husbandry PR #122). The
  textures here are the original colours scaled to 512 px.
- **Gait and bake: PZA** (`mod/tools/froxlotl/bake_froxlotl.py`, built on PZA's Kenshi Motion Lab): a salamander walk and run,
  baked into `froxlotl.pzaf` (rest, walk and run frames).
